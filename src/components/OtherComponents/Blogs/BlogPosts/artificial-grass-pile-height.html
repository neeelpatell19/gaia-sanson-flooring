Pile height is the length of the grass blades above the backing, and it decides most of what an artificial lawn feels like. A tall pile looks lush and feels soft underfoot but flattens where people walk most. A short pile looks neat, sweeps easily and stands up to chairs and wheels, but feels firmer. In Indian homes and projects the same question comes up again and again: how tall should the grass be? The answer depends far more on how the area will be used than on how it looks in a sample.

What is pile height in artificial grass?

The height of the grass yarn measured from the top of the backing to the tip of the blade, usually given in millimetres. GAIA Landscape Grass is named by its pile height: SFL 20 has a 20 mm pile, SFL 60 a 60 mm pile, and the range climbs in 5 mm steps between them, giving nine heights in all. Every height uses the same two-tone green, UV-resistant yarn. Pile height is not the whole story: density, blade shape and yarn type change how a lawn feels, which is why two 35 mm grasses from different makers can look and wear differently.

Which pile height is best for a balcony or terrace?

A short one, usually 20 to 35 mm. Balconies and terraces carry chairs, tables, planters and constant footfall in a small area, and a short pile stands up to that without flattening into tracks. It also sweeps and rinses quickly, which matters in dusty months, and it suits the scale of a small space. Of the nine GAIA Landscape Grass heights, the shorter end, SFL 20 to SFL 35, is usually the practical choice. What height suits a garden lawn?

For a lawn that looks and feels soft, 40 mm and above. A fuller pile gives the deep, natural look most homeowners want, and in a larger garden it has room to look its best. It needs more brushing to keep the blades upright in walkways, and it holds more dust and leaves, so plan a short brushing routine. Where children play and run, a mid-height pile between 30 and 40 mm balances softness with resilience. The full range is shown under GAIA Landscape Grass.

What pile height do sports surfaces need?

Shorter than lawns, and specified by the sport. Tennis, badminton, skating and multi-sport courts need a short, dense surface so balls bounce predictably and wheels roll freely; GAIA MultiTurf comes in 10 mm and 15 mm pile heights for exactly that. Football needs a longer pile that holds infill, which cushions players and controls how the ball rolls; GAIA football turf comes in 30 mm and 50 mm. Competition football surfaces are tested under the FIFA Quality Programme for football turf, which shows how much the sports world relies on measured performance rather than looks. Does a taller pile get hotter or fade faster?

Heat and fading depend far more on sun exposure and yarn quality than on height. Any artificial grass warms up in direct sun, so shade over the areas people use is the real fix, as explained in does artificial grass get hot. Fading depends on UV resistance: the yarn in GAIA Landscape Grass is certified to UNE 14836, the Spanish edition of the European method for testing how synthetic turf yarn holds up to UV exposure, set out in the EN 14836 standard.

How does pile height change maintenance?

Taller piles need more brushing; shorter piles need less. Long blades lean and mat in walkways and under furniture, so they benefit from a stiff brush against the grain every few weeks. Short piles mostly need sweeping and rinsing. Either way, remove leaves and litter regularly and rinse during dusty spells. The Synthetic Turf Council's guidelines set out the same principle for every surface: routine upkeep is what keeps turf performing. A full routine is in how to clean artificial grass.

Pile height checklist

  • Plan shade for any area used on hot afternoons.

Pile height questions

What is the most popular artificial grass height for homes?

For balconies and terraces, a short pile of 20 to 35 mm. For garden lawns, 35 to 45 mm is a common balance between a soft look and good wear.

Is 40 mm artificial grass too long?

Not for a garden lawn with moderate use. It will need regular brushing in walkways, and a shorter pile suits balconies, play areas with wheels and spaces with furniture.

Which pile height is best for pets?

A short to mid pile, around 20 to 35 mm, is easier to rinse and keep clean. Good drainage underneath matters more than height.

What pile height is used for football turf?

GAIA football turf comes in 30 mm and 50 mm. The 30 mm plays firmer and faster; the 50 mm gives a fuller, more cushioned surface once it is filled.
