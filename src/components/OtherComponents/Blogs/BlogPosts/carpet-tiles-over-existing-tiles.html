Most Indian homes and offices already have a hard floor: vitrified tiles, ceramic, marble or Kota stone. Breaking it out is noisy, dusty, slow and expensive, and it can damage the waterproofing or services beneath. Laying carpet tiles straight over the top avoids all of that, and it is one of the reasons carpet tiles suit renovations and rented spaces. It works well when the old floor is prepared properly. This article explains what to check, how to prepare and where to take care.

Carpet tiles over existing tiles at a glance

  • Sound: every old tile firmly bonded, none loose, hollow or cracked.
  • Level: grout lines filled flush and any lips ground down.
  • Clean: wax, polish and sealers removed so the adhesive can grip.
  • Dry: no damp rising through the floor, especially at ground level.
  • Edges: doors, thresholds and skirting adjusted for the new floor height.

Can you lay carpet tiles over ceramic or vitrified tiles?

Yes, and it is a common way to carpet an existing building. A tiled floor is usually hard, flat and stable, which is what a carpet tile needs beneath it. The Carpet and Rug Institute's CRI 104 installation standard covers laying carpet over existing ceramic tile: surface finishes are removed, the surface is abraded, grout lines are filled flush with the tile surface, and a primer may be needed. Polished vitrified tiles are smooth and non-porous, so preparation is what makes the adhesive hold.

How do you prepare an old tiled floor?

Work through it in this order.

  1. Check every tile. Tap the floor and listen for hollow sounds. Lift and re-fix or replace any loose, hollow or cracked tiles, because they will move and show through.
  2. Remove finishes. Strip any wax, polish or sealer, then clean and degrease the floor.
  3. Fill grout lines. Deep or wide grout joints will print through a carpet tile over time. Fill them flush with a suitable levelling compound.
  4. Level the high spots. Grind down lips between tiles and patch any dips.
  5. Prime and lay. Apply the primer the adhesive maker recommends, then the tackifier, and lay the carpet tiles.

Do grout lines show through carpet tiles?

They can, if they are left open. A carpet tile is flexible enough to follow small dips, and a regular grid of sunken grout joints can appear as faint lines on the surface after months of foot traffic, particularly on low-pile tiles and under raking light from a window. Filling the joints flush before laying removes the problem. Large-format vitrified tiles with narrow joints need less work than small ceramic tiles with wide ones.

What about damp floors?

Test before laying, especially on ground floors and in older buildings. Moisture rising through a slab gets trapped under carpet tiles, can weaken the adhesive and can encourage mould and smells. CRI 104 requires moisture and alkalinity testing before carpet tiles are glued down, including relative humidity testing of concrete slabs under ASTM F2170, and the same caution applies over old tiles, since grout joints are porous. In monsoon months, fix the source of any leak and let the floor dry fully before laying. Bathrooms, kitchens and any room that is washed down regularly are not suited to carpet.

Living rooms, reading corners and home offices can be carpeted without breaking out the old floor.

Will carpet tiles raise the floor height?

A little. GAIA carpet tiles are 5 to 5.75 mm high, depending on the collection, so plan for that at doors and where the carpet meets another floor. Doors may need trimming to clear the new surface, and a threshold strip gives a neat, safe edge where carpet meets tile, marble or timber. Check the clearance under wardrobes and fitted furniture too.

Carpet tiles over existing tiles questions

Do I need to remove old tiles before laying carpet tiles?

Not if they are sound, level and dry. Loose, cracked or damp tiles must be fixed before laying.

Can carpet tiles be laid on marble or Kota stone?

Yes, with the same preparation: remove polish, fill joints, level high spots and use a primer the adhesive maker approves.

Can carpet tiles be removed later without damaging the tiles beneath?

Carpet tiles laid on a releasable tackifier lift off. Some adhesive residue and any filler in the grout lines will need cleaning off afterwards.

How long does it take to carpet over an old tiled floor?

Preparation usually takes longer than laying. Once the old tiles are sound, filled and primed, an average office floor or a few rooms at home can be laid in a day or two.

Can carpet tiles go over underfloor heating?

Ask before ordering. The backing, adhesive and heating system must be compatible, and the heating maker's limits on floor coverings apply.
