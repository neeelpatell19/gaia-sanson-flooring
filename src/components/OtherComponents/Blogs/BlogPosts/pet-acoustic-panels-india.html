What are PET acoustic panels made of?

Polyethylene terephthalate fibre, the same polymer family as plastic bottles, formed into a dense, felt-like board. In GAIA PET acoustic panels the fibres are recycled polyester bonded with heat, so the board holds together without chemical binders or glues. The open fibrous structure is what absorbs sound: sound waves enter the board, lose energy as friction among the fibres, and less sound is reflected back into the room. The same structure makes the panels light and easy to cut on site, far more durable than acoustic foam, and resistant to moisture, insects and mould, which matters in humid coastal cities and during the monsoon.

How do you compare sound absorption between suppliers?

Ask for the NRC, the test method and the mounting it was measured on. NRC, the noise reduction coefficient, runs from 0 (reflects everything) to 1 (absorbs everything). It is measured in a reverberation room under ASTM C423 or ISO 354, and the result depends heavily on how the sample was mounted. The same GAIA board rates NRC 0.3 glued flat to masonry and NRC 0.85–0.9 when it is mounted with an air gap behind it. A supplier that quotes only the higher figure, with no mounting stated, is comparing a different installation from the one most projects will build. European suppliers often add an absorption class from A to E under ISO 11654; ask for that too if your consultant works to it. Our explainer on what NRC means covers how to read the numbers.

Which thickness and sheet weight should you ask for?

The thickness that suits the room, and the sheet weight that proves the board is dense enough. GAIA PET panels are made in 9, 12, 18 and 24 mm thicknesses. A 9 mm GAIA sheet weighs 1,650 g/m² and a 12 mm sheet 2,250 g/m², and other weights and sheet lengths can be produced to order. Two 12 mm boards from different suppliers can perform differently if one is much lighter, so compare grams per square metre, not thickness alone. Thicker boards absorb more of the lower speech frequencies, which is why studios, auditoriums and home theatres tend to specify 18 or 24 mm, while 9 and 12 mm suit offices, classrooms and feature walls. The trade-offs between sizes are set out in 9, 12, 18 or 24 mm panels.

How do you check a green claim?

Ask for the certificate, then read it. GreenPro is India's Type 1 ecolabel for building and interior products, run by CII's Green Business Centre and accredited by the Global Ecolabelling Network. A genuine certificate names the product, its thickness or type, the manufacturer, the plant and an expiry date. If any of those is missing, or the product named is not the one being quoted, the claim does not cover your order.

What about fire safety?

Ask for the fire test report and the standard it was tested to, and check it with the project's fire consultant. Fire requirements depend on the building type, the location of the panels and the local authority, and India's National Building Code sets the framework most projects follow. A supplier should be able to name the test, the laboratory and the product tested, in the same way it names the product on a green certificate. PET acoustic panel questions

Should I choose PET panels or acoustic foam?

For most interiors, PET. The panels are denser, hold their shape, can be printed or carved, and GAIA's are GreenPro certified. Foam suits small studios on a tight budget. The full comparison is in our article on PET panels and acoustic foam.

What size are PET acoustic panels?

GAIA PET acoustic panels come as 1220 × 2440 mm sheets in 9, 12, 18 and 24 mm thicknesses. They can be cut on site into tiles, strips or shapes, and other sheet weights and lengths can be made to order.

Do PET acoustic panels soundproof a room?

No. They absorb sound inside a room, cutting echo and noise build-up, but they do not stop sound travelling through walls. Blocking sound between rooms needs mass and airtight construction.

How long do PET acoustic panels last?

Indoors and out of direct sun, many years. The board does not crumble like foam, and surface dust can be vacuumed off with a soft brush.
